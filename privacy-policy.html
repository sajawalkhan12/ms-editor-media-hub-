Privacy Policy

MS Editor Media Hub

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MS Editor Media Hub (the “App” and its related website) handles information when you use the service. The service lets users browse media and prompts, create an account, publish posts, and interact with posts.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, username, and authentication/session information when you register or sign in.
  • Profile information you choose to provide: display name, biography, location text, profile photo, and cover image.
  • Content you submit: post titles, descriptions, prompts, media files, poster images, and external media links.
  • Community interactions: likes and comments. If you comment, the name you enter and the comment text are submitted with the post.
  • Basic app preferences: the App stores session tokens and a randomly generated visitor identifier locally on your device to support sign-in and remember whether you have liked a post.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, maintain login sessions, display profiles and posts, store and deliver uploaded media, show likes and comments, and provide the App's features. We do not use the App to request your contacts or precise device location.

3. Public and private posts

Posts marked Public, public profile details, and comments may be visible to other users and may be copied or shared outside the service. Only choose to publish information you are comfortable making public.

Posts marked Private are intended to be visible only to the account owner through the service's access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you paste an external media link, that link may remain publicly accessible on the third-party provider even when your post is marked private. Uploading the media file directly is preferable when you need the service's private-post controls.

4. Service providers and storage

The service uses Supabase for account authentication, database services, and media storage. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. These providers process information as needed to operate their services and are subject to their own privacy and security terms. When you open an external media link, the third-party site may collect information under its own policy.

5. Sharing and advertising

We do not sell your personal information. We do not currently display advertising in the App. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, disclosed where required by law, or made visible when you choose to publish public content.

6. Data security and retention

We use the access controls and security features provided by the App's infrastructure. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Account and submitted content may be retained while your account or the relevant content remains active, and for a reasonable period where needed for security, legal, or operational purposes.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You can choose what profile details and posts to submit, avoid publishing sensitive information, and sign out of the App. Signing out does not delete your account or previously submitted content. To request account or personal-data deletion, contact the developer at sijawal333@gmail.com from your account email and include your username. Do not send your password. We may need to verify that you control the account before processing a request. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

8. Children's privacy

The service is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so the information can be reviewed.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with its effective date updated accordingly.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email sijawal333@gmail.com.
